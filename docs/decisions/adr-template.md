## Context

⟨What situation forces a decision? State the constraint or conflict, not the solution.
Two to four sentences. Include any hard numbers that bound the choice — token limits,
memory budgets, dimensionality, latency targets.⟩

## Decision

⟨One or two sentences. What we will do. Present tense, definite.⟩

## Alternatives considered

⟨The most important section. Each alternative gets: what it is, and why it was rejected.
Where a rejection rests on evidence from the literature, cite it.⟩
